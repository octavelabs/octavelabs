Logg — Helping Nigerians relocate with confidence through a digital platform built around clarity, guidance, and trust.

Timeline — 8 weeks

Visit site ↗
  • Product Strategy
  • Web Development
  • UI/UX Design
  • UX Research

Opportunity

Relocating to the UK is a fragmented experience. Information is scattered across multiple websites, applicants struggle to understand the right pathway, and service providers often operate without transparency. Logg set out to simplify the journey by bringing guidance, trusted partners, and progress tracking into one cohesive platform.

View design ↗View design ↗View design ↗

Understanding
the Users

Applicants need a clear starting point, a manageable plan, and people they can trust. These needs shaped the experience from the first question to the next milestone.

[01]

Users didn’t know where to start.

Many prospective applicants knew they wanted to relocate but lacked clarity on the most suitable pathway.

[02]

The process felt overwhelming.

Requirements, timelines, and documentation differed across visa routes, making progress difficult to track.

[03]

Trust was difficult to establish.

Applicants wanted confidence that advisors and service providers were credible before committing.

Solution

A connected experience that starts with the right questions. Guided onboarding helps applicants explore their pathway, a personal dashboard brings tasks and documents together, and a partner workspace keeps support organised around each client’s journey.
