Opportunity

Knowledge becomes more engaging when there is a reason to put it into practice. Sabiplay brings learning and friendly competition into one experience, giving players a place to test what they know, discover challenges, and see their progress.

A Home for Every Player

A clear dashboard connects the player’s profile, XP, competitions, and rankings. The next challenge is always easy to find.

View design ↗

Understanding
the Users

Players arrive with different levels of confidence. The experience needs to welcome curious newcomers while giving competitive players meaningful goals.

[01]

An easy place to start.

A recognisable home screen and clear navigation help players understand where to go and what to try next.

[02]

A challenge worth returning to.

Competition discovery and visible progress give each visit direction, from exploring a challenge to checking a ranking.

[03]

Progress that feels personal.

Avatars, XP, and campus and national leaderboards bring identity and a sense of belonging to the experience.

Solution

A bright, approachable platform that connects individual progress with a wider community of players. Familiar dashboard patterns make the product easy to navigate, while expressive imagery, warm yellow accents, and playful competition cards give Sabiplay its personality.
