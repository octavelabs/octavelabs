Opportunity

A familiar property game can feel entirely different when the streets, humour, and surroundings feel like home. Landlord brings that idea into the browser: a playful city-building rivalry that is easy to start alone and designed to share with friends.

A City with Personality

From Lagos and Abuja to London and Manchester, city selection gives each game a distinct setting. Local references and playful descriptions make choosing a board part of the experience.

View design ↗

Understanding
the Players

Some players want a quick solo game. Others want an evening of rivalry with their friends. Both need a clear route into play and enough flexibility to make the game their own.

[01]

Start without the wait.

Bot opponents provide an immediate way to play, with easy, normal, and hard difficulty choices.

[02]

Bring your own people.

Online play introduces room codes and shared links, making it straightforward to organise a game with friends.

[03]

Keep the next move clear.

A readable player list, prominent action controls, and focused property details help players make decisions around a rich 3D board.

Solution

A colourful, characterful game that combines a 3D city board with a straightforward interface. Players choose a city, set their opponents and house rules, then move between buying, building, and collecting rent. English and Pidgin language options extend the game’s local identity beyond its streets.

The Lagos board places the game inside a lively streetscape, with traffic, buildings, and familiar neighbourhoods. Player balances and the current action stay close at hand while the board takes centre stage.

Your Game, Your Rules

Make Every Property Count

A focused title deed explains the purchase price, rent progression, building costs, and mortgage values. The board remains visible behind it, keeping the decision connected to the game.
