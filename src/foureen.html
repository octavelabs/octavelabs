Opportunity

Finding something abroad is the easy part. Understanding the full cost, arranging delivery, and knowing what happens next can be harder. Foureen connects shoppers in Nigeria with products from the UK and US through a service built around clear information and a straightforward journey.

From Wishlist to Doorstep

The service is explained before a customer makes a request. Four clear steps connect item discovery, a complete quote, approval, and delivery.

View design ↗

Understanding
the Users

Customers need more than access to overseas stores. They need enough clarity to decide whether a purchase makes sense, and an easy way to ask for help.

[01]

Know the whole price.

An item’s sticker price is only part of the decision. Shipping, service fees, and local delivery need to be visible and understandable.

[02]

Find the right route.

Some shoppers know exactly what they want; others prefer to browse. A catalogue and a custom request flow support both starting points.

[03]

Stay connected after ordering.

A dedicated tracking entry point and clear contact options give customers a way back to their order and the people handling it.

Solution

A shopping experience that puts clarity first. The homepage introduces the service and an illustrative cost estimate. The shop pairs delivered totals with local comparisons, while the request form captures the details needed for a personalised quote. A calm visual identity brings those routes together.
